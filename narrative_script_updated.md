Well, I was digging around because I know so many artists want to get their work in front of people during that chaos without spending a fortune, especially if you're into digital or crypto stuff.

Grab your coffee, because I found a couple of solid free opportunities that are still open, and they actually include you being exhibited, with entry to the event vibes.

First one that jumped out: One Love Art DAO. They're a non-profit global art collective with a digital gallery at the Hotel Yve in Downtown Miami, right by Biscayne and that big Ferris wheel. Their open call is called FRONTERA. They're looking for works that challenge our understanding and shift perspectives. Digital, physical, NFT or not, minted on any chain or unminted, all welcome. Global artists, no problem. Selected pieces get exhibited at their gallery at Hotel Yve during Miami Art Week, and considered for additional partner opportunities. Completely free to apply and participate. Deadline is November 15th, 2026. They've already got over a hundred responses and the quality is high, but form is still open as of early November. Google Form: https://forms.gle/DjERA61QPdZEf19SA or just search "One Love Art DAO FRONTERA open call". Application seems visual-focused; they collect the art from selected artists. From what artists are sharing, it's straightforward – name, work, statement-ish, links. Encouraging because it's community-driven Web3-ish DAO energy without barriers.

Second: Render Network's Miami After Dark. This is for digital artists – they're selecting 10 to 15 for a 50-foot large-scale projection exhibition on Miami Beach on December 2nd, 2026, at The National Hotel. Theme is Miami After Dark. Works up to 2 minutes long, and they have to be rendered on the Render Network, but they cover the rendering costs with credits for selected artists. No application fee. It's a chance to get your digital work projected huge during Art Week, with that decentralized GPU tech story. Deadline also November 15th, 2026. Application is a Typeform with about 9 questions: full name, then probably contact, portfolio links, the work itself, bio or statement, etc. Takes about 7 minutes. Search "Render Network Miami After Dark open call" or go to renderfoundation.typeform.com/to/NGgLRjhY. Some notes say US artists preferred or only, so double-check, but it's free and high-visibility.

These are perfect for that special segment – clear deadlines, no cost, actual exhibition during the week, and crypto/tech adjacent for the Web3 crowd. People can search our database later for "MIAMI" and pull the open ones with URLs.

If you hear of more free ones, especially from curators or orgs doing digital during the week, drop them in. Keep applying, your work deserves that spotlight. You've got this!
