### 1. One Love Art DAO – “FRONTERA” at Hotel YVE (Web3 / DAO sweet spot)
This one feels made for our people. One Love Art DAO (a nonprofit 501(c)(3) with a permanent digital gallery presence) is calling for work that “challenges our understanding and shifts perspectives.” Selected artists get exhibited in their gallery at Hotel YVE during Miami Art Week and considered for additional partner opportunities.

**Deadline:** November 15, 2026  
**Cost:** Free to apply and exhibit (they’re community-first).  
**What they want:** Original works that push boundaries—digital, physical, minted or unminted. Theme is open as long as it makes people see differently.  
**When / Where exhibited:** During Miami Art Week (Nov 30–Dec 6) at the art gallery inside Hotel YVE, Miami.  
**Entry:** Yes—selected artists are part of the activation.  
**How to apply / details:** Google Form → https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdqPVMlUMsAbZOW3v48Z8tacY-nSzYWsSUckDA_2ryFQ17H3g/viewform  
Or search “One Love Art DAO Frontera Miami” or check @OneLoveArtDAO on X.  
**Application load:** Roughly 8–12 questions (name, contact, short bio, artist statement, Instagram/website, theme fit, links or uploads).  
**Receipts needed:** Typically 3–5 portfolio pieces or links + bio + short statement.

This is the one I’d jump on first if you have something that feels fresh and perspective-shifting. They’ve been active and responsive on X.

### 2. Nova Gallery – Miami Art Week 2026 Online Exhibition via Artsy
If you want collector eyes and a platform that actually sells, this is worth the modest fee. Nova Gallery is selecting artists for an online exhibition presented through Artsy right in the middle of Art Basel Miami Beach energy.

**Deadline:** November 20–26, 2026 (ArtConnect lists Nov 26 UTC; gallery materials lean toward Nov 20–25—check the latest).  
**Fee:** $40 USD.  
**Why the fee is justified:** You get a real Artsy listing during the biggest art week of the year, a dedicated press release distributed to 75,000+ contacts, presentation to their collectors and art advisors, 70% of net sales, and possible invitation to 12-month non-exclusive representation. That’s exposure + sales path that pure free calls rarely deliver.  
**When / Where:** Online via Artsy, Dec 3, 2026 – Jan 5, 2027.  
**What they want:** Strong visual work across painting, sculpture, photography, digital, new media, mixed media, etc.—anything with presence and collector appeal.  
**Details:** https://www.artconnect.com/opportunity/gOFrOmD67OnaUQ_VOTjrH or thenovagallery.art  
**Application load:** Payment first, then email with up to 5 images + confirmation.  
**Receipts:** Up to 5 images + short supporting info.

### 3. Black & White Gallery – Red Dot Miami 2026 (Monochrome / Sculpture)
Red Dot is a respected satellite fair running December 2–6. The Black & White Gallery is doing a juried search specifically for monochrome photography and freestanding contemporary sculpture that will show in their pavilion.

**Deadline:** November 10, 2026 (tight—act fast).  
**Fee:** $45.  
**Why include it:** Red Dot Miami is a solid, established fair during Art Week. Getting into a curated monochrome presentation there puts you in front of real collectors in a focused context.  
**When / Where:** Red Dot Miami, Dec 2–6, 2026 (Mana Wynwood area).  
**Details:** Search “Black & White Gallery Red Dot Miami 2026” or check EntryThingy / CaFE listings for Miami.  
**Application load:** Standard juried entry—images, statement, CV-ish info.  
**Receipts:** Portfolio images (usually 3–10) + bio/statement.
