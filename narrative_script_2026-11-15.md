# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment Script
## Miami Art Week Free Exhibition Opportunities – November 15, 2026

**Voice:** Encouraging, uplifting, casual like a friend over coffee at the café. Authoritative but never punching down. Celebrating artists stepping into the spotlight.

---

[Coffee shop ambiance. Soft clinks of cups. Warm, inviting tone.]

Hey, you. Pull up a chair. Miami Art Week is right around the corner — November 30 through December 6 — and the whole city turns into this massive, buzzing canvas of galleries, projections, and pop-ups. Art Basel is the big ticketed thing, but the real magic for so many of us is the free and open opportunities where your work can actually hang, project, or get seen by the global crowd without breaking the bank.

Today I want to highlight one that’s still open and perfectly timed, especially if you’re into digital, physical, or that Web3 energy. It’s free to the artist, includes entry into the events, and it’s run by a nonprofit DAO that actually lives this stuff.

**One Love Art DAO – FRONTERA at Hotel Yve**

They’re looking for works that challenge our understanding and shift perspectives. Theme is FRONTERA — borders, edges, flipping how we see things. Selected artists get exhibited in their gallery at the YVE Hotel Miami, right downtown, during the whole Art Week. There’s also an AR Art Walk on December 3rd (meet at the hotel at 2 PM, walk to Bayfront Park), open studios, receptions, and partner opportunities. Physical or digital, minted or unminted, any chain — they’re cool with it. Global artists welcome, and you don’t have to be there in person for digital showings.

Deadline: Originally November 15th (that’s today!), but they’ve been running a flash call too — aim to share by the end of this weekend if you’re just seeing this. It’s free. No application fee. Participation is free.

What they’re looking for: Original contemporary work — painting, digital, photography, mixed, anything that fits the theme of shifting perspectives.

How to apply: Super simple. Either fill out the Google form (search “One Love Art DAO FRONTERA Miami Art Week form” or go straight to the link they posted: forms.gle/DjERA61QPdZEf19SA) or post on X: follow @OneLoveArtDAO and @FidelEverywhere / @BrunchByFidel, share ONE piece of art, tag a friend. That’s it for the flash version. 

Application details: Minimal questions — basically contact info, a short bit about the work or you, and the art. Receipts: 1 portfolio piece / image or link is the core. Maybe a quick bio or statement if the form asks. No long CV required.

Event: Exhibited December 1–6 at Hotel Yve (146 Biscayne Blvd area). AR walk Dec 3. Full FRONTERA programming all week. Entry to the gallery and related events is included for selected artists.

URL for details: Check their X @OneLoveArtDAO, or the site onelovedao.org, or the Luma event page for FRONTERA. Google “One Love Art DAO FRONTERA Hotel Yve Miami Art Week 2026” and you’ll land right on it.

This one feels special because it’s artist-led, nonprofit, and genuinely open to the crypto/digital community that’s been building these bridges for years. Your work gets seen by collectors, curators, and the Art Week crowd without you paying a booth fee.

If this one just closed on you, don’t stress — keep your eye on their X and Instagram for last-minute spots, and we’ll keep updating the database. There are always more doors opening.

You’ve got this. Go submit that piece that flips someone’s view. The city is waiting.

[Warm laugh. Sip of coffee.]

What are you putting up for FRONTERA? Tell me. We’ll celebrate when it’s on the wall.

**End of segment.**
