# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment: Miami Art Week Exhibition Opportunities

**Narrator (casual friend over coffee voice):** Hey, grab a seat — this coffee’s on me. I’ve been digging around for you, and there are some really exciting ways to get your work out there during Miami Art Week this year. No gatekeeping, just real opportunities that put artists front and center. The week runs roughly November 30 to December 6, 2026, with Art Basel Miami Beach public days December 4-6. Here’s the good stuff, encouraging and clear.

First up, and this one is pure gold for digital and web3 folks: the Render Network’s Miami After Dark open call. Deadline is coming up fast — sources point to October 30 or shortly after, so jump on the Typeform today. They’re looking for original digital works, up to about 2 minutes, themed around “Miami After Dark.” Selected artists (10-15 of them) get their pieces projected large-scale — think 50-foot wall — on Miami Beach on December 2, 2026, right in the heart of Art Week at a spot like the National Hotel. It’s free to apply, no fee at all. Plus, they cover rendering costs with Render Network credits, and the work has to be rendered on their decentralized network. Full credit to you, exposure to the massive Art Basel crowd. It’s being exhibited publicly during the week. Details and apply at https://renderfoundation.typeform.com/to/NGgLRjhY — Google “Render Network Miami After Dark open call” if you want more context. From what I can tell, the form is short, takes about 7 minutes: probably your name, contact, a short bio or statement, description of the work, and links to portfolio or the piece itself. A few questions, a couple of receipts (links/portfolio). Super accessible.

Next, if you want something more physical and inclusive: Fridge Art Fair. Deadline October 31, 2026. They’re accepting applications for individual works (one per entry) for their Miami Beach edition December 1-6, 2026, across several South Beach venues. Theme vibes with “Gianni • Madonna • Coconuts” — fun, nostalgic, artist-driven. It’s $35 per entry, which covers the fair production and keeps it accessible and independent. Everyone’s welcome, especially LGBTQ+ artists and allies. You get to exhibit in this playful, alternative satellite fair that people actually go to, and public admission has been free or low in past years. Apply via their CaFÉ listing or fridgeartfair.com/submissions. Google “Fridge Art Fair Miami 2026 open call.” Application is straightforward: image of the work, basic info. One main receipt (the artwork image) plus details. The fee is justified because it funds the real, on-the-ground exhibition space during peak week without the big corporate markup.

One more with a modest fee for the online prestige: Nova Gallery’s Miami Art Week 2026 Online Exhibition via Artsy. Deadline November 20, 2026. $40 application fee. They select artists for a show running roughly Dec 3, 2026 to Jan 5, 2027, presented during Art Basel Miami Beach on Artsy — one of the top platforms collectors actually browse. Possible consideration for representation. Open to contemporary media including new media. Submit up to 5 images. The fee gets you in front of a serious collector audience with zero participation fee after that, and Artsy visibility is genuinely valuable. Details on ArtConnect or search “Nova Gallery Miami Art Week Artsy.” A handful of questions, portfolio images as the main receipts.

These are solid, uplifting paths to get exhibited without breaking the bank. The Render one especially shines for web3 and digital artists. Check the links, apply soon where deadlines are tight, and remember — your work deserves to be seen. I’ll keep updating the database so we can surface fresh ones daily.
