Hey, so grab your coffee — or whatever’s in that cup — and let me catch you up on what’s still open for exhibiting during Miami Art Week this year.

## Opportunity 1: Downtown Miami Art Week 2026 – Rolling Registration

This one is happening right now. Downtown Miami Art Week, fourth edition, across three galleries on East Flagler Street: Aura Copeland Gallery at 209, Les Couleurs Art Collective at 125, and Aura Copeland Contemporary at 129. Exhibition December 1–6, VIP Preview November 30, 7–10 PM. Curated by Gabrielly Paz, sponsored by Mana Common. They’re looking for emerging and established contemporary artists — physical work mostly, paintings, sculpture, etc. It’s a professionally presented multi-space show with public hours and private appointments.

**Deadline:** Rolling / check availability soon, because Art Week is right around the corner and delivery windows were earlier in November.

**What they’re looking for:** Original contemporary visual art. You register by reserving wall space.

**Application details:** Registration is via their MoonClerk link on the site. From past years and guidelines, there is a fee for the wall space reservation (it covers the professional presentation and installation). Why the fee? You get curated placement in three gallery spaces during the peak of Art Week, VIP preview access, sales opportunity (they take a commission), and real foot traffic from collectors and visitors. Prestigious local activation in the historic Flagler District.

**How many questions / materials:** Registration form for space size + artist info. Then you deliver physical work with inventory sheet, photos of each piece, labels (name, title, medium, dimensions, price), and hanging hardware. Roughly bio/contact + 3–5 pieces typical, plus the physical receipts.

**URL / where to go:** https://www.lescouleursmiamibeach.com/artweek 
Google: “Downtown Miami Art Week 2026 artist registration” or email Lcmiamiarts@outlook.com / curator Gabrielly Paz at contact@gabriellypaz.com.

It’s not free, but if you want a real physical gallery presence right in the middle of the week, this is one of the clearer remaining paths.

## Opportunity 2: From Miami Publications – January 2027 Submissions

Deadline: Friday, December 4, 2026. They accept art, photography, writing, and comics. While not a pure Art Week exhibition, it’s Miami-focused and the deadline falls right at the end of the week, so selected work could connect to the energy.

**What they’re looking for:** Local/Miami-inspired visual and literary work.

**Fee:** Appears free (publication open call).

**Materials:** Check their Instagram @frommiamipublications for exact form. Typically portfolio images, bio, statement.

**Where:** Search Instagram @frommiamipublications or Google “From Miami publications submissions”.

## Opportunity 3: Port Everglades Cruise Terminal 25 Mural – Public Art

Deadline: Monday, December 21, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. For South Florida-based artists. This is a permanent public mural commission at the cruise terminal — high visibility for thousands of travelers.

**What they’re looking for:** Mural proposals that fit the terminal environment.

**Fee:** Free to apply (public art call).

**Materials:** Likely proposal, images of past work, bio, concept. Standard public art package.

**Where:** Broward County Public Art / Port Everglades channels. Google “Port Everglades Terminal 25 mural open call 2026”.
