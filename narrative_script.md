# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment: Miami Art Week Exhibition Opportunities

**[Café conversation style – encouraging, uplifting, authoritative friend over coffee]**

Hey, so you’re thinking about showing some work during Miami Art Week this year? Perfect timing. The whole city’s about to light up from around November 30 through December 6, 2026, with Art Basel Miami Beach right in the middle of it December 4–6. Collectors, curators, crypto folks, everyone is going to be there. I’ve been digging around for real opportunities where you can actually exhibit—digital or physical—without the usual pay-to-play walls, especially ones that lean into Web3, digital, or just open the door for emerging voices.

Here’s the good stuff I’m seeing right now. These are clear, with real deadlines, and the selected artists get exhibited and usually get into the event itself.

First up, the one that jumped out for digital and tech-leaning artists: Render Network’s “Miami After Dark.” They’re selecting 10 to 15 artists for a big 50-foot large-scale projection exhibition in Miami Beach on December 2, 2026, at The National Hotel. Theme is “Miami After Dark.” No fee at all. They even sponsor the rendering costs with Render Network credits if your piece needs it—work has to be rendered on their network, up to 2 minutes long. Open mainly to U.S. artists. Application is a short Typeform, takes about 7 minutes. Deadline is November 15, 2026. Head to the Typeform (search “Render Network Miami After Dark Typeform” or go through rendernetwork.com / ArtDeadline) and submit. This is pure Web3-adjacent decentralized GPU power meeting Miami nights. Super cool, zero cost, and your work gets projected big during the week.

Next, something really prestigious and completely free to enter: the inaugural .ART Award. They’re celebrating ten years of the .ART domain. No submission fee—you just need a .ART domain (they often make claiming one free or easy through partners like HUG). Your website or profile linked to that .ART URL *is* the application. You need a bio of at least 100 words, documentation of the work (images, video, or 3D with medium/materials/dimensions), and a short story of the process (100+ words or a 30-second to 1-minute video). Open to everyone 18+, any medium, any country. Deadline November 1, 2026. Semi-finalists November 6, finalists November 20, winners announced December 3 right at Art Basel Miami Beach. Prizes include $15,000 cash grand prize, residencies in France, Spain, and Georgia, editorial coverage, a premium .ART domain, Tezos prize, Times Square screens later, and more—over $50k total. There’s even an opt-in for gallery visibility at BitBasel in Miami. Jury includes Jerry Saltz and strong voices from the digital and contemporary side. Go to award.art or hug.art for the details. This is the kind of recognition that actually moves careers, and it’s free.

Then there’s Art Miami Magazine’s ongoing open call for artist interviews. Completely free, rolling basis. Email them 6 high-res photos of your work (with title, medium, size, year), a 200–400 word art statement, and your short bio plus contact and Instagram or website. They feature selected artists online, on social, and consider them for special print editions during key Miami events like Art Week. All mediums, any country. Send to their submissions email (check artmiamimagazine.com/open-call-for-artist-interviews/). Not a physical exhibition guarantee, but solid editorial exposure right when the eyes of the world are on Miami, and it can lead to more.

If you’re open to a small fee for official status, Miami Art Week’s own arteNXT Digital Exhibition is worth a look. There’s an application fee, then if accepted the digital package is around $279 (or $399 for multi-city). You get the Official 2026 arteNXT Digital Exhibition Artist designation, ongoing presence through October 2027, potential promotional features during the week, membership, and access to the broader ARTWEEK ecosystem. Deadline to be considered for the 2026 Miami promo is October 31, 2026. Apply at miamiartweek.org. The fee buys real official branding and year-round visibility in a curated digital show tied to the biggest week of the year—prestigious if you’re building a professional presence.

There are others with fees like Nova Gallery’s Artsy online exhibition ($40 app fee, deadline November 20, possible representation consideration), but I’m prioritizing the free and high-value ones first.

We’re building a living database of these so we can drop 1–3 fresh ones regularly and you can search by keyword like “Miami” or “digital” or “Web3.” Deadlines get marked so we know what’s still open. Always double-check the official URL because things move fast.

You’ve got this. The work is already there—these are just the doors opening during the biggest art week of the year. Grab a coffee, pick one, and submit. Which one feels right for what you’re making?
