# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment: Miami Art Week Exhibition Opportunities

**Style:** Casual friend over coffee at the café – authoritative but uplifting, encouraging, never punching down.

---

Hey, so you're sitting there with your oat milk latte, and I'm telling you straight: Miami Art Week is coming up fast – November 30 through December 6, 2026, with Art Basel right in the middle December 4-6. The whole city turns into this massive art playground, and yes, there are still ways for artists like us to get our work in front of people without breaking the bank.

I'm not going to sugarcoat it – pure free open calls with clear deadlines for physical or digital exhibition slots during the week are getting scarce this late in the game. A lot closed in October. But a couple of solid ones are still open or just wrapping, and some with small fees that actually get you something prestigious. Let's walk through them like we're planning your next move.

First up, and this one's time-sensitive because the deadline is today, November 10: The Black & White Gallery at Red Dot Miami. They're looking for black-and-white photography (strictly mono) and freestanding contemporary sculpture. Event is December 2-6 at Mana Wynwood, right in the heart of Miami Art Week satellite fairs. Your work gets exhibited physically on the fair floor if you're the grand prize winner – full space fee waived, dedicated wall or footprint, curatorial management, and Artsy representation. Even runners-up get digital exhibition and roster inclusion. Fee is $45 for 3-5 images or $55 for 6-10. Why the fee? It covers juried image processing and the secure entry system – and the prize is a fully subsidized physical spot at a real Art Week fair, which is pretty prestigious. Application: pay first, then upload high-res images, include your website or Instagram. That's basically a handful of materials – the images plus socials/bio links. About 2-3 main fields after payment. Go to theblackandwhite.gallery or search "Black & White Gallery Red Dot Miami open call". URL: https://theblackandwhite.gallery/open-call-the-black-white-gallery/ or the apply link https://theblackandwhite.gallery/product/open-call-miami-art-week/

Next, Nova Gallery's Miami Art Week 2026 online exhibition. Deadline around November 20-26. They're taking painting, sculpture, photography, mixed media, digital art, conceptual, installation, video, printmaking, textiles, ceramics, drawing, collage – pretty much everything. It's exhibited online via Artsy during Art Basel Miami Beach, December 3, 2026 to January 5, 2027. Selected artists may get considered for representation. Fee $40. Why? Application processing, and you get real Artsy exposure to collectors, 70% of net sales, press to 75k+ contacts. Materials: up to 5 images, artist statement, CV. A few questions on the form. Search "Nova Gallery Miami Art Week ArtConnect" or go to artconnect.com for the opportunity.

Then there's One Love Art DAO's FRONTERA open call. Deadline was November 15, form may have closed (check X @OneLoveArtDAO), but it's a Web3/crypto related nonprofit DAO with a digital art gallery at Hotel YVE in Miami. Looking for works that challenge understanding and shift perspectives. Selected artists exhibited at their gallery during Miami Art Week, plus possible partner opportunities. Seems free – no fee mentioned. Materials: visual submissions, probably images and short description. Perfect for digital/NFT artists. Google "OneLoveArtDAO FRONTERA Miami" or check their X posts from late October.

Also worth watching: FAMESPACE at Red Dot Miami booth 600, December 2-6, open submissions for creatives, but involves membership ($125) then exhibition costs – not free, but free tickets for attendees via email list on famespace.co. And remember, lots of free public art to experience once you're there: No Vacancy in hotels (Nov 12-Dec 19, free entry), Collins Park outdoor sculptures, ICA Miami free admission, etc.

The point is, your work deserves to be seen. These are real pathways into the biggest art week of the year. Check the URLs, Google the names if links change, and if one fits, submit today. You've got this – the art world needs more voices like yours.

For the full searchable database, head to the ArtVision GitHub or search Miami + keyword + URL in our running list. Deadlines update daily.

That's the scoop over coffee. What are you thinking of submitting?
