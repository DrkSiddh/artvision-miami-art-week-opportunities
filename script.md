# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment Script: Miami Art Week 2026 Exhibition Opportunities
# Narrative format - Friend telling you everything over coffee at the café
# Casual but authoritative. Encouraging, uplifting, never punching down.
# For the ArtVision brand.

Hey, grab a seat — the coffee’s still hot. I’ve been digging around for you, and there are some genuinely good openings right now for artists who want to get their work in front of people during Miami Art Week. No gatekeeping energy here. Just clear paths, especially if you’re working digital or connected to the Web3 side of things.

Miami Art Week is November 30 through December 6, 2026. Art Basel Miami Beach public days are the 4th through the 6th, but the whole city is buzzing with satellite shows, hotel galleries, projections, and public installations. If your work is strong and you’re ready to share it, there are ways in without emptying your pockets.

First one that jumped out: One Love Art DAO is running an open call called FRONTERA. They’re looking for work that challenges how we see things and shifts perspective. It’s open to global artists, digital or physical, minted or not, any chain. Selected pieces go up in their gallery space at the YVE Hotel in Downtown Miami during the week, roughly December 1–6, and there can be other partner opportunities too. It’s free. Completely free. Deadline is November 15th. Head to their form — search “One Love Art DAO FRONTERA Miami” or go straight to the Google Form they’ve been sharing on X under @OneLoveArtDAO. You’ll probably need a short bio, a statement, and a few links or images of the work. It’s a nonprofit collective built by and for artists, so the vibe is community-first. If the form glitches, just reach out on X. They’re responsive.

Second, if you’re a digital artist who loves scale: Render Network has been calling for work under the theme “Miami After Dark.” They’re selecting a small group for a big 50-foot projection on Miami Beach on December 2nd. Rendering costs are covered with credits if you’re chosen. No application fee. Some listings say U.S. artists only, so double-check. Deadline also looks like November 15th in a few places (there were earlier dates floating around, so verify today). Search “Render Network Miami After Dark open call” or check their Typeform. You’ll submit the digital piece itself plus the usual bio and statement. It’s a real architectural moment during the week — the kind of thing people actually stop and look at.

If public space speaks to you more than a gallery wall, SaveArtSpace has “SO THEY SAY” up. Curated for bus shelter ads in Miami, opening around December 30th (right after the main week). They’re asking for work that plays with advertising, identity, and what we’re sold every day. Deadline November 23rd. There’s a $35 fee for a few images, which goes toward actually buying the ad space and producing the installation — it’s tax-deductible and the visibility is street-level real. Apply at saveartspace.org/so-they-say. Same idea: images, bio, statement.

There’s also an open call from Aura Copeland Gallery for Downtown Miami Art Week across a few Flagler Street spaces, December 1–6. Artists from Miami and abroad are welcome. Contact curator Gabrielly Paz at contact@gabriellypaz.com or check their Instagram @auracopeland_gallery for the registration link. Costs aren’t crystal clear yet, so ask directly before you invest time.

The pattern is clear: deadlines are tight (mid-to-late November), most want a bio, statement, and a handful of strong images or links, and the free or low-barrier ones are out there if you move. Your work is already good enough to be seen. These are just doors. Pick the one that feels like your language, submit clean, and then go make more.

We’ll keep adding to this list so you can search “MIAMI” plus whatever keyword matters to you and see what’s still open. You’ve got this. Now finish that coffee and go apply to one.
