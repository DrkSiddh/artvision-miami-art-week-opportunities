# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment Script: Miami Art Week Opportunities

**Voice: Casual, encouraging, uplifting friend over coffee at the café. Authoritative but warm. Never punching down. Focus on free or high-value opportunities with clear directives.**

Hey, pull up a chair — coffee’s on me. I’ve been digging through the noise around Miami Art Week (that’s November 30 to December 6, 2026, right around Art Basel Miami Beach) and I found a couple of real ones that actually give artists a shot without draining your wallet. We’re talking clear deadlines, actual exhibition, and entry into the event. Perfect for our ArtVision special segment on free and Web3-friendly opportunities.

First up: **One Love Art DAO’s FRONTERA open call.** These folks are a nonprofit 501(c)(3) global art collective with a permanent digital gallery at the YVE Hotel in Miami. Theme is FRONTERA — works that challenge how we understand things and shift perspectives. Selected artists get exhibited in their gallery at Hotel YVE during Miami Art Week, plus consideration for other partner activations that week. 

It’s completely free. Open to global artists. Digital or physical, minted or unminted, any chain or marketplace. Deadline is November 15, 2026. They’re looking for original work that fits the theme. Apply via the Google form: https://forms.gle/DjERA61QPdZEf19SA (or just search “One Love Art DAO FRONTERA Miami Art Week”). 

Application side: It’s a straightforward form — expect basic contact info, artist bio, a short statement on the work, and links or uploads of your pieces (probably 1–3 images or URLs). Not a mountain of paperwork. Event includes gallery exhibition and broader visibility during the week. Super Web3-friendly since they’re a DAO. If this lights you up, get that form in before the 15th. You’ve got this.

Next: **The .ART Award on HUG.** This one’s prestigious and free. Your website (or HUG artist profile connected to a free .ART domain) *is* the application — no traditional form, no fee. Over $50,000 in prizes: cash, editorial coverage, residencies, and more. Jury includes heavy hitters like Jerry Saltz. Winners announced live at Art Basel Miami Beach on December 3, 2026. Open to every discipline, worldwide.

They’re looking for the process, practice, and story behind the work. Details at hug.art/open-calls/ARTaward or just Google “.ART Award HUG Art Basel Miami.” Application is essentially your online presence — so have a solid portfolio site or HUG profile ready. Questions are minimal because the site itself is the submission; receipts are basically your site + any supporting materials you highlight. This is high-prestige recognition during the biggest week of the year. Totally worth it if you’ve been building online.

Bonus note: If you want pure exhibition and can swing a small fee for a really good reason, keep an eye on things like Nova Gallery’s Artsy online exhibition (around $40 fee for online presence during the week), but we prioritize the free ones. Always double-check the URL yourself.

That’s the run for today. Search our database with “MIAMI” plus the keyword and URL — only open ones will show. Deadlines move, so we update daily. Go create something that shifts a perspective. Coffee’s getting cold — your turn to share what you’re working on.
